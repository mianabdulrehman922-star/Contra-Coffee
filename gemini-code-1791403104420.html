THE CONTRA EXPERIENCE

Your Second Home.

Contra Coffee was built on a singular belief: that exceptional specialty coffee belongs alongside thoughtful food and intentional architecture. Located in the heart of Gulberg 2, Lahore, Contra is a sanctuary designed for morning rituals, deep conversations, quiet productivity, and evening catch-ups.

From precision-poured espresso to handcrafted sourdough sandwiches and small-batch bakes, everything we serve is prepared with craft, care, and attention to detail.

"Fine brews, blends & bakes — crafted for people who appreciate the finer details."
